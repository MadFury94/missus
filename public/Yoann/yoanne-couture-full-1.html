Our Story

An old dye, worn new.

"Adire has always been a language — we simply gave it a modern accent."

Founded in Lekki Phase 1, Yoanne Couture began with a single conviction: that Adire, Nigeria's centuries-old resist-dye textile, deserved a place in the everyday wardrobe of the contemporary woman — not only in archives and galleries. Every piece is hand-finished, cut for movement, and dyed in small, deliberate batches.

Our atelier works directly with indigo dyers across the Southwest, pairing their craft with premium bases — silk-cotton blends, brushed linen, and hand-beaten cotton — so that heritage feels like luxury, not costume.

Description
A floor-length wrap kaftan cut from silk-cotton Adire, hand-dyed in the house's signature indigo butterfly resist pattern. Fitted through the bodice with a self-tie wrap waist and a soft column skirt that moves easily from day into evening. Fully lined.
Fabric & Care
70% cotton, 30% silk. Hand-dyed using traditional wax-resist Adire technique — minor variation in pattern is part of the handcraft, not a flaw. Hand wash cold in mild detergent, do not wring, dry flat away from direct sun, iron on reverse at low heat.
Shipping & Delivery
Dispatched from our Lekki Phase 1 atelier within 24–48 hours. Lagos delivery in 3–5 working days; nationwide delivery in 5–8 working days. Express Lekki/Ikoyi same-day delivery available on request via WhatsApp.
Size & Fit
Model is 5'8" wearing a size S. True to size through the bust and waist with room through the skirt. Custom lengths and sleeve adjustments available — select "Custom" and note your measurements at checkout.

FAQ

Do you offer custom sizing?
Yes — select "Custom" under size and share your measurements over WhatsApp after checkout, or book an in-atelier fitting in Lekki Phase 1.
Will the Adire pattern match the photos exactly?
Each piece is hand-dyed, so pattern placement varies slightly from the photos — this variation is part of the handcraft and is not considered a defect.
Can I pay on delivery?
Pay on delivery is available within Lagos for orders confirmed via WhatsApp. Nationwide orders require payment in advance.
